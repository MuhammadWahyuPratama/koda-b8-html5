Indonesia's Pioneer of Gluten Free food products since 2013, Ladang Lima provides tasty and healthy gluten free foods from locally sourced cassava into your table. Ladang Lima produces cassava flour through biotechnology methods that create characteristics similar to wheat flour. Besides cassava flour, Ladang Lima also offers gluten free noodles, cookies, pasta, and other healthy products that support a better lifestyle.
